# Ringi Request (Template)

Hoshimori Kogyo Co., Ltd.

A ringi request is how a proposal gets approved in our company: the proposer writes the request, it is passed through
each level in turn, each level stamps its approval, and it finally reaches the person with the authority to decide.

| Item | What to write |
|---|---|
| Subject | Introduction of (name of system or service) |
| Proposer | Department, full name |
| Date of proposal | Year, month, day |
| Approval level | President / Division head / Department manager |

## 1. Purpose and background

Why this is needed. Show the current problem with figures.

## 2. Content

What will be introduced, the scope, the period, and who will do the work.

## 3. Costs

Initial cost, monthly cost, and cost effectiveness (in how many months the investment is recovered).

## 4. Risks and countermeasures

Information security, impact on daily work, and the criteria for stopping if the project fails.

## 5. Routing order

Information Systems Department, then International Sales Department, then the executive in charge, then the President.

Note: before routing the request, explain it to the related departments in advance and obtain their agreement
(nemawashi).

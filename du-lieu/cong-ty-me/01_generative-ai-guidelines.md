# Generative AI Usage Guidelines (Version 1.2)

Hoshimori Kogyo Co., Ltd., Information Systems Department
Issued: April 1, 2025. Revised: July 1, 2026.
Scope: Hoshimori Kogyo Co., Ltd. and its overseas subsidiaries (including Hoshimori Vietnam Co., Ltd.).

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines set the basic rules for using generative AI to work more efficiently, so that we avoid information
leaks, infringement of rights, and damage caused by incorrect information.

## 2. Approved services

1. Use only services that the company has contracted and the Information Systems Department has approved. The list of
   approved services is on the internal portal.
2. Do not enter business information into free services used with a personal account.
3. To use a new service for business, obtain approval in advance through a ringi request.

## 3. Information that must not be entered

- Drawings and specifications received from customers under a non-disclosure agreement
- Personal information (names, contact details, evaluations, and so on, of employees and of contact persons at business
  partners), except when you use an approved service, state the purpose, and have your department head's approval
- Unpublished information on selling prices, costs, and profits

## 4. Using AI output

1. The person in charge must review the content of any AI output before using it for business.
2. When AI was used for a document submitted to a customer or to the parent company, record who reviewed it.
3. Do not let AI alone decide on accepting orders, shipping products, or judging quality.

## 5. Where data is stored

When an overseas subsidiary uses a service located outside Japan and sends data to the head office in Japan, check the
personal data protection laws of its own country and consult the Information Systems Department.

## 6. Violations

If you violate these guidelines, or see a risk of a violation, report it immediately to your department head and to
the Information Systems Department.

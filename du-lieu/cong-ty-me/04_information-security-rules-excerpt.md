# Information Security Rules (Excerpt)

Hoshimori Kogyo Co., Ltd., Edition 4 (revised October 2024)

## Article 12 (Classification of information)

Company information is classified into three levels:

1. Strictly confidential: customer drawings, costs, information on new products not yet announced
2. Internal: production plans, order information, quality records, employee information
3. Public: company profile, published product catalogs

## Article 15 (Taking information outside or sending it outside)

1. Do not store or send strictly confidential information on systems outside the company.
2. Internal information may be handled on an outside system only if the Information Systems Department has approved
   that system.
3. When personal information is sent or received between an overseas subsidiary and the head office, follow the laws
   of the sending country and complete the required procedures.

## Article 18 (Access rights)

Grant access rights to business systems only to the minimum extent needed for the job, and remove them promptly when
the person leaves the company or moves to another department.

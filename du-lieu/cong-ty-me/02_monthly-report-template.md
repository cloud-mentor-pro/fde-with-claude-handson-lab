# Monthly Report Template for Overseas Subsidiaries

Hoshimori Kogyo Co., Ltd., International Sales Department

Submit to: International Sales Department, Hoshimori Kogyo. Deadline: 17:00 Japan time on the third business day of the
following month.
Format: write in English following this template, and attach the actual results table in Excel.

## 1. Production results

- Production volume by item code, compared with the plan
- Equipment utilization (for each molding machine and wire harness line)

## 2. Quality

- In-process defect rate (target: 1.0% or less) and the main defect types
- Number of customer complaints, their content, and the status of countermeasures

## 3. Issues and countermeasures

Write each issue in this order: current situation, cause, countermeasure, deadline, person in charge.

## 4. Plan for next month

- Production plan and expected large orders
- Requests for support from the head office

## Notes for writing

- Every figure must match the attached actual results table.
- Avoid vague statements such as "we will do our best"; give concrete figures and deadlines.
